Roof repairs, leaks and storm damage

If your roof is leaking, call us. We find where the water is getting in, fix that, and tell you what shape the rest of the roof is in.

What we repair

  • Leaks around flashings, valleys and skylights
  • Loose, lifted or rusted sheets
  • Storm and wind damage
  • Rusted or leaking gutters and downpipes
  • Capping and flashings that have come loose

After a storm

If a storm has lifted sheets or put a branch through the roof, call 0405 876 410. Send us photos if you can take them safely from the ground, and please stay off a wet roof. If water is coming in and you need emergency help, the NSW SES is on 132 500.

Maintenance

Roofs last longer when someone looks at them now and then. We do maintenance visits for homeowners, landlords and commercial sites, where we check the sheets, fixings, flashings and gutters and fix the small things before they turn into leaks.

Questions

My roof is leaking right now. What should I do?

Put a bucket under it, move what you can, and call or text us on 0405 876 410. We're open Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Outside those hours, text us photos and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Will my insurance cover it?

That depends on your policy. We can give you a written quote and photos of the damage to send to your insurer.

Can you tell me if the roof needs replacing?

Yes. While we are up there we look at the whole roof, and we'll tell you if a repair will hold or if it's time to plan for a new one.
