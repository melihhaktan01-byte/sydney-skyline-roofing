Services

Roofing for builders and new homes

A good part of our work is for builders: single homes, duplexes and small estates. We quote from the plans, turn up when the frame is ready and get the roof on so your other trades can get inside.

Call 0405 876 410Get a free quote

One trade for the whole roof

One crew does the sheets, flashings, gutters, fascia, downpipes and skylights, so you are booking one trade for the roof. We are licensed and insured, and we can send our insurance details with the quote.

Recent builds

In Warnervale on the Central Coast we roofed three new homes in the same estate. Closer to home we have done new duplexes in Merrylands, Bass Hill, Chester Hill and Yagoona, and new homes in Chifley, Maroubra and Caringbah.

Photos at handover

When a roof is finished we often put the drone up and photograph it. You get clear shots of the whole roof for your records and for your client, and nobody has to climb a ladder to check it.

Questions

Do you travel outside Sydney?

Yes, for the right job. We have roofed homes on the Central Coast and we take on work in regional NSW.

Can you price a multi-home job?

Yes. Send the plans for each lot and we'll price them together.

How much notice do you need?

The more the better. Send the plans when you have them and tell us when the frame is due, and we'll hold the dates.
