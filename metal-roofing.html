New metal and COLORBOND® roofs

Most of what we do is metal. We sheet new homes, duplexes, granny flats and extensions across Sydney in COLORBOND® steel, and we do the flashings, capping and gutters ourselves, so the whole roof is one job with one crew.

What goes into a metal roof

A metal roof is more than the sheets. Under them go the insulation blanket and sarking. On top go the ridge capping, barge capping, valleys and the flashings around anything that comes through the roof, like vents, skylights and antenna mounts. That finishing work decides whether a roof stays dry, so that's where we slow down.

The roof isn't finished until the capping is complete.Sydney Skyline Roofing, on Instagram

Profiles and colours

Corrugated is the profile most people picture, and it suits most pitched roofs. Low-pitch and flat roofs need a deeper profile, such as a trapezoidal or concealed-fix sheet, so the water gets away properly. We'll tell you which profiles suit the pitch of your roof, and then you pick the colour from the COLORBOND® steel range.

If you are matching a neighbour's roof or a builder's spec, send us the colour name and we'll quote on that.

Insulation and ventilation

We can lay insulation blanket under the sheets while the roof is open, which is the easiest time to do it. We also fit roof vents where a roof space needs to breathe.

Questions

How long does a new metal roof take?

It depends on the size of the roof, how many hips and valleys it has, and the weather. A simple single-storey roof moves a lot faster than a two-storey home with skylights and box gutters. We'll give you a start date and a rough finish date with the quote.

Can you quote from plans?

Yes. For new builds and extensions, send us the roof plan and we can quote from that, then confirm on site once the frame is up.

Do you supply the materials?

Yes. Our sheets, flashings and gutters come from No1 Roofing & Building Supplies, and the quote covers supply and install.
