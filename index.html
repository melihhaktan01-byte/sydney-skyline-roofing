  Questions
What people ask before they book

  
Are quotes free?
Yes. Call, text or send the form and we'll quote the job at no cost.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Sydney Skyline Roofing Pty Ltd is licensed and insured, and our ABN is 54 679 555 670.
Which areas do you cover?
All of Sydney. We are based in Chester Hill and our recent jobs run from the Eastern Suburbs to the Shire and Western Sydney. We have also worked on the Central Coast and we take on jobs in regional NSW.
Do you work for builders?
Yes. We roof single homes, duplexes and multi-lot jobs for builders, and we can quote from the plans.
Do you only do metal roofs?
Metal is most of our work. We also fit skylights in tiled roofs.
Can I send photos first?
Yes. Text photos of the roof and your suburb to 0405 876 410. Often we can tell you what we are looking at before we come out.
Where do your materials come from?
Our sheets, flashings and gutters come from No1 Roofing & Building Supplies, our roofs are COLORBOND® steel, and our skylights are VELUX.
