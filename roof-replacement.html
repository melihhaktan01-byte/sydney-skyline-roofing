Roof replacement and re-roofing

When an old roof has had enough, patching it gets expensive. We strip tired tile and metal roofs and put a new metal roof in their place, with new flashings and gutters so you aren't back fixing the edges a year later.

Call 0405 876 410Get a free quote

Signs it's time

  • Tiles that are cracked, slipping or going porous
  • Rust coming through old metal sheets, especially around the screws and overlaps
  • Leaks that come back after they have been repaired
  • A ridge line that sags
  • Water stains on the ceiling after heavy rain

One of these on its own can usually be repaired. A few of them together on an old roof usually means the money is better spent on a new one. We'll tell you which way we would go if it were our house.

Tile to metal

A lot of older Sydney homes have concrete or terracotta tiles. Metal is much lighter on the frame than tile, and it has far fewer joins for water to find. We take the tiles and old battens off, check the timber, fit new battens to suit the sheets, then sheet and flash the roof.

Living in the house while it's done

You can stay home for most re-roofs. We strip and sheet in sections where we can so the house isn't left sitting open, and we check the forecast before we pull anything off.

Questions

Should I repair or replace?

If the roof is sound and the problem is one flashing or a few sheets, we'll repair it. If the sheets or tiles are at the end of their life, repairs only buy time, and we would rather tell you that up front.

Do you take the old roof away?

Yes. Stripping, loading and disposal are part of the job.

Can you add skylights or insulation at the same time?

Yes, and it's the best time to do both, because the roof is already open.
